Liquidation mechanism calculation (BETA):

Start price of token in pool = 100 USDC

Let's open bid position:
Price: 95
Size: 5
Leverage: 4

Margin will be = (price*size)/leverage = 118.75 USDC


After that price will fell down to 80 USDC
Losses will be:

Avaible_margin = position_margin - ((position_price-new_token_price)*position_size)
Avaible_margin = 118.75 - ((95 - 80)*5) = 43.75

Default maintaince percentage is 25%. Let's calculate current level of position after losses

percentage = (avaible_margin*100)/(new_token_price*position_size)
percentage = (43.75*100)/(80*5) = 10%

It's less than 25%, so position will be liquidated
